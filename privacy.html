Last Updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eyooss Bot handles information when you use our Discord bot.

Depending on the features you use, Eyooss Bot may process the following information:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server IDs.
  • Channel IDs.

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate bot features.

  • Manage server configurations.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the bot, comply with legal obligations, or protect security.

Information is retained only for as long as needed to provide the bot's features or meet legal and security requirements.

Users or server administrators may contact us to request deletion of information associated with their use of the bot.

6. Children's Privacy

Eyooss Bot is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.
